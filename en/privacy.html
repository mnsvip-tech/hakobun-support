Article 1 — Service Overview

HAKOBUN ("the App") is a delivery management support app designed to improve the efficiency of delivery drivers' work. It provides features such as parcel registration, route management, delivery status tracking, and OCR-based address scanning. The App is a business support tool. Compliance with laws, safe driving, and adherence to your delivery company's rules during actual delivery operations remain your own responsibility.

Article 2 — Eligibility

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over. Please use it as a delivery driver or as a manager of delivery operations. Using the App requires registering a profile within the app (name, base location, contact information, email address). This registration is a local profile stored only on your device; linking a Google account or external authentication is not required. Registration with false information is prohibited.

Article 4 — Information We Collect and Its Purpose

■ Location Data (GPS) Purpose: Navigation to delivery destinations, current location display, and route optimization Collection timing: Only while the app is in use (no background collection) Destination: Current location and destination coordinates are sent to the Google Directions API for route calculation ■ Camera / Images Purpose: Automatic input of address and recipient name via OCR scanning of shipping labels Processing: Processed on-device (iOS: Vision framework / Android: Google ML Kit) and never sent to external servers Storage: Temporary images are promptly deleted after recognition is complete ■ Voice (Microphone) Purpose: Voice-to-text input for addresses, memos, and status updates Processing: Uses the speech recognition service configured on your device (system default). Depending on implementation, voice data may be processed remotely by that service provider ■ Address / Postal Code Purpose: Coordinate conversion (geocoding) and address lookup from postal codes Destination: Google Geocoding API (address → coordinates), zipcloud (postal code → address, a third-party postal code lookup service) ■ Input Information (Name, Phone Number, Email Address) Purpose: Management of delivery operations and driver profile settings Storage: Encrypted storage on your device (iOS: Keychain / Android: EncryptedSharedPreferences, AES-256-GCM) External transmission: None (except for the address and location data described above)
